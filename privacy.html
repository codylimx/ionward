1 Introduction

IONWARD LTD. (UEN: to be registered) is a company incorporated in Singapore. This Privacy Policy applies to our website ionward.com, our services, and all interactions you have with us.

We are committed to protecting your personal data and complying with the Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA) of Singapore, as amended by the Personal Data Protection (Amendment) Act 2020, and all applicable subsidiary legislation and guidelines issued by the Personal Data Protection Commission (PDPC).

By using our website or services, you consent to the collection, use, and disclosure of your personal data as described in this Policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue using our services.

2 Personal Data We Collect

We may collect the following categories of personal data:

Category Examples How Collected
Identity Data Full name, job title, company name Forms, email, meetings
Contact Data Email address, phone number, WhatsApp Contact forms, WhatsApp chat
Professional Data CV/résumé, LinkedIn profile, technical skills Job applications, email
Usage Data IP address, browser type, pages visited, referral URL Automatically via cookies / analytics

We collect personal data only with your knowledge and, where required, consent. We do not collect sensitive personal data (e.g., NRIC numbers, race, religion) unless strictly necessary and with explicit consent.

3 Purposes of Collection & Use

We collect and use your personal data for the following purposes, which are notified to you at or before the point of collection:

  • To respond to enquiries — including project enquiries, partnership requests, and general questions submitted via our website or WhatsApp.
  • To evaluate job applications — reviewing CVs and profiles for open roles or future opportunities.
  • To deliver services — onboarding clients, managing software development engagements, and staff augmentation.
  • To manage contractual relationships — billing, invoicing, and contract administration.
  • To improve our website and services — using aggregated analytics to understand how visitors use our site.
  • To comply with legal obligations — including Singapore's PDPA, Accounting and Corporate Regulatory Authority (ACRA) requirements, and any applicable MAS regulations.
  • To send business communications — project updates, newsletters, or marketing (only with your consent; you may opt out at any time).

We will not use your personal data for purposes beyond those listed above without obtaining fresh consent or where permitted by the PDPA.

4 Disclosure of Personal Data

We may share your personal data with the following categories of recipients, strictly on a need-to-know basis:

  • Service Providers — third-party vendors who assist us in operating our website and services (e.g., cloud hosting, email platforms, analytics). These providers are contractually bound to protect your data.
  • Business Partners — where required to fulfil a service engagement you have requested, such as subcontractors or specialist engineers.
  • Group Companies — any subsidiaries or affiliates of IONWARD LTD. for internal business operations.
  • Legal & Regulatory Authorities — government agencies, courts, or regulators in Singapore or elsewhere where required by law or to defend legal claims.
  • Professional Advisors — lawyers, auditors, and accountants bound by professional confidentiality obligations.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties for their marketing purposes.

5 Retention of Personal Data

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by law:

  • Client and contract records — 7 years from end of engagement (per Singapore's Limitation Act and ACRA requirements).
  • Job applications (unsuccessful) — up to 2 years, unless you consent to a longer period.
  • Website enquiries — up to 3 years or until the purpose is fulfilled.
  • Marketing communications — until you withdraw consent or opt out.

When personal data is no longer required, we will securely destroy or anonymise it in accordance with our data retention procedures.

6 International Data Transfers

IONWARD LTD. operates with team members in Singapore and Vietnam. Your personal data may be transferred to, stored in, and processed in Vietnam or other countries.

Where we transfer personal data outside Singapore, we comply with the PDPA's transfer limitation obligation by ensuring that the recipient country provides a standard of protection at least comparable to Singapore's PDPA, or we put in place binding contractual arrangements (data transfer agreements) with the recipient.

⚠️ Note: By engaging with us and providing your personal data, you acknowledge that your data may be processed in Vietnam under appropriate safeguards.

7 Security of Personal Data

We implement reasonable and appropriate technical and organisational security measures to protect your personal data from unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction, including:

  • Encrypted data transmission (TLS/HTTPS) for all web communications.
  • Access controls and role-based permissions limiting who can access personal data.
  • Regular security assessments and code reviews.
  • Staff training on data protection obligations.

However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. In the event of a data breach that is likely to result in significant harm, we will notify affected individuals and the PDPC in accordance with the Mandatory Data Breach Notification obligation under the PDPA Amendment Act 2020, within 3 calendar days of assessment.

8 Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar tracking technologies to improve your experience and collect usage analytics. Types of cookies we may use:

  • Strictly Necessary Cookies — required for the website to function; cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics Cookies — help us understand how visitors interact with our site (e.g., Google Analytics, used in anonymised/aggregated form).
  • Preference Cookies — remember your settings and preferences.

You may control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality. Where required by applicable law, we will obtain your consent before placing non-essential cookies.

9 Your Rights Under the PDPA

Under Singapore's PDPA, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you and information on how it has been used or disclosed in the past 1 year.
  • Right of Correction — request that we correct any inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent — withdraw consent at any time for purposes based on consent, with reasonable notice. Withdrawal does not affect processing prior to withdrawal.
  • Right to Data Portability (where applicable) — request that we transmit your personal data in a structured, machine-readable format to another organisation, where technically feasible.
  • Right to Erasure / Cease Use — in certain circumstances, request that we stop using or erase your personal data.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact our Data Protection Officer (DPO) as detailed in Section 12. We will respond within 30 days of receiving a valid request. We may charge a reasonable fee for access requests where permitted by law.

10 Children's Personal Data

Our website and services are directed at businesses and professionals aged 18 years and above. We do not knowingly collect personal data from individuals under the age of 18. If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected personal data from a child, we will take steps to delete it promptly.

11 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, legal requirements, or business operations. The updated Policy will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.

For material changes, we will provide more prominent notice (e.g., via email or a website banner). Your continued use of our services after any changes constitutes acceptance of the revised Policy.
